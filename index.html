How do I find the total length of a YouTube playlist?+

Paste a YouTube playlist link or ID into the calculator and select Calculate watch time. Playlist Clock adds the available video durations and shows the total in hours, minutes, and seconds. Multiple links get a combined total and an individual breakdown.

How does playback speed change watch time?+

Watch time is the original duration divided by playback speed. A six-hour playlist takes four hours at 1.5× and three hours at 2×. Use a preset or enter a custom speed from 0.25× to 16×. These are estimates; your player’s supported speeds may differ.

Can I calculate a range or only the videos I have left?+

Yes. Open Fine-tune your calculation and choose A range to enter the first and last video numbers. Choose Remaining and enter the video number you have watched through. Include the current video if you still need to finish it. These settings apply separately to every playlist entered.

Do private playlists or live streams work?+

Public and accessible unlisted playlists work. Private playlists require account authorization and are not supported. Deleted, private, ongoing live, upcoming, and missing-duration videos are excluded and reported. Regional restrictions can also affect playback availability.

How is my finish date calculated?+

Your remaining duration at the selected speed is divided into daily watching sessions. The planner counts only your chosen weekdays, using your browser’s local date. Videos marked as already watched are removed from the estimate. Ads, breaks, and pauses are not included.

Is it free, and what happens if the quota runs out?+

The calculator is free and requires no account. It uses the YouTube Data API, which has a daily quota. If that quota is exhausted, try again after the reset or enter your own restricted API key in the optional settings.

What data is stored?+

Your theme choice is saved in this browser. Links, results, and any personal API key stay in this tab’s memory and are cleared when you reload. Requests are sent directly to Google to retrieve video information. YouTube thumbnails also load from Google. This site does not use analytics or advertising cookies.
